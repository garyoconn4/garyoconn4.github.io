Privacy, in plain English

StudyTime is used by students who are under or around 18, so we keep as little as possible and keep it private.

What we keep

Subjects, topics and how confident each student feels about them, the study sessions they plan and finish, results they or a parent type in, past papers, flashcards, project milestones, rewards and messages between family members.

Where it lives

In one Google Sheet owned by the family's own Google account. Nobody else has a copy. There are no adverts and no tracking.

Who sees what

Each student sees only their own things. Parents see how each child's study is going and can suggest sessions, add dates and set rewards. Parents cannot delete a child's sessions, read their flashcards or see their wellbeing check ins.

Wellbeing check ins

Private to the student by default. A student can choose to share a weekly summary. If stress is high several days running the app suggests a break or a chat and links to Jigsaw and SpunOut. It never alerts parents unless the student has chosen to share.

PINs and passwords

They are scrambled (salted and hashed) before they are stored, so nobody can read them, not even whoever runs the Sheet. Five wrong tries locks an account for 15 minutes.

Emails

Students get a Sunday "week ahead" email and parents a Sunday summary. Each person can switch any email off in settings.

Removing data

Ask the family admin and every row belonging to a person can be deleted from the Sheet.
